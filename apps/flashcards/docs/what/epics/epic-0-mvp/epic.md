# Epic 0 — MVP Flashcard App (World Capitals)

## Goal

MVP flashcard app with World Capitals theme, flip mode, and session scoring that enables structured memorization with immediate feedback.

## Business Value

Enable structured memorization with immediate feedback — learners can practice geography recall in focused sessions and track their progress without needing accounts or backend infrastructure.

## Scope

- **Home screen**: theme/mode selection (World Capitals pre-loaded, flip mode)
- **Session screen**: card flip interaction, score tracking (Know / Don't Know buttons), progress indicator
- **Results screen**: session summary with score, total cards, accuracy %, replay button
