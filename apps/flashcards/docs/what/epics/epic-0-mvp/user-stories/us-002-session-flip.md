# US-002 — Session Card Flip Interaction

## Story

As a learner, I want to see a card and flip it to reveal the answer so that I can test my knowledge.

## Expected Behavior

Session screen displays a flashcard showing a country name. Tapping or clicking the card flips it to reveal the capital. A progress bar shows session advancement. After flip, "I knew it" and "I didn't know" buttons appear.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Card shows country name
- Tap/click flips to show capital
- Progress bar visible
- "I knew it" / "I didn't know" buttons appear after flip
