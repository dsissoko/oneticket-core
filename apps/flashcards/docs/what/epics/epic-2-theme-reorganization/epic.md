# Epic 2 — Theme Reorganization: World Capitals into 12 Themes

## Goal

Reorganize the single "World Capitals" theme (currently 12 random capitals) into 12 distinct themed datasets organized by geography and economic alliances, enabling learners to study capitals in focused, meaningful categories.

## Business Value

Learners can now study capitals by continent, alliance, or economic ranking — providing structured, contextual learning paths instead of random sampling. This supports targeted study (e.g., "I have a test on African capitals") and progressive learning (start with one continent, expand to others).

## Scope

- Define 12 themed datasets: Africa, Antarctica, Asia, Europe East, Europe West, North America, South America, Australia, BRICS Alliance, NATO Alliance, 20 biggest GDP, 20 lowest GDP
- Each theme contains relevant country-capital pairs
- Update theme picker on home screen to display all 12 themes
- Ensure session flow works correctly with any selected theme
- Maintain existing flip mode and scoring functionality
