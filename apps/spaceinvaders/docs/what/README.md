# What

The `what/` section describes the product and business needs.

It defines:
- the product vision,
- the users and actors,
- the business concepts,
- the expected capabilities,
- the workflows,
- the business rules,
- the epics and user stories.

This section describes:
- what the system must achieve,
- for whom,
- and why.

It does not describe the technical implementation.

Typical artifacts:
- product specification,
- epics,
- user stories,
- acceptance-oriented functional scope.

The `what/` section is intentionally technology-agnostic.
